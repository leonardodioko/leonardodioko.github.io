04

The build

Two columns, in Buffett's construction: what the business earns, capitalised — and what sits on the balance sheet that the business does not need to earn it.
05

Which assumption moves the answer

Each assumption nudged up and down by a sensible step, sorted by how far the answer travels. A row reading zero is an assumption that is switched off or does not matter — worth knowing either way.
06

Reverse test

Rather than asking what the company is worth, ask what the market's price already assumes. This is the discipline that survives without a price history: if the implied figures are ones you would happily underwrite, the discount is real.
